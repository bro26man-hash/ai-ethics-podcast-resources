# AI Ethics Podcast — Curated Resources

A crowdsourced collection of open-source projects, tools, and research for the *AI Ethics & Social Justice* podcast.

---

## 🤖 Algorithmic Fairness & Bias Auditing Toolkits

| Project | Maintainer | Stars | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Trusted-AI/AIF360**](https://github.com/Trusted-AI/AIF360) | IBM / Linux Foundation AI | ⭐ 2,865 | A comprehensive set of fairness metrics for datasets and ML models, explanations for those metrics, and algorithms to mitigate bias. Industry-standard toolkit. |
| [**fairlearn/fairlearn**](https://github.com/fairlearn/fairlearn) | Microsoft | ⭐ 2,285 | A Python package to assess and improve fairness of ML models, featuring the Reductions library for constraint-based fairness optimization. |
| [**ResponsiblyAI/responsibly**](https://github.com/ResponsiblyAI/responsibly) | Shlomi Hod | ⭐ 101 | Toolkit for auditing and mitigating bias and fairness of ML systems, with particular focus on NLP models. Aligned with the *Fairness and Machine Learning* textbook. |
| [**Cognitivelens-AI-Human-Comparison**](https://github.com/AmirhosseinHonardoust/Cognitivelens-AI-Human-Comparison) | Amir Hossein Honardoust | ⭐ 20 | Streamlit-powered analytics tool for exploring alignment between human and AI decisions — fairness, calibration, and interpretability. |
| [**bias-fairness-system**](https://github.com/Adeniran2020/bias-fairness-system) | Adeniran | — | Modular system for detecting algorithmic bias and auditing fairness in ML workflows (Adult Census income benchmark). |

## 📚 Foundational Reading

- **Fairness and Machine Learning** — Solon Barocas, Moritz Hardt & Arvind Narayanan ([fairmlbook.org](https://fairmlbook.org))
- **"Speciesist bias in AI"** — Hagendorff, Bossert, Tse & Singer (2023). *AI and Ethics*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-023-00380-w)
- **SpeciesismBench** — Hagendorff et al. (2025). Testing LLM harm toward farmed animals.
- **AHA Benchmark** — AI-for-Animals (2025). Species-dependent risks of harm in LLM outputs.
